# Lumen — Future Feature Ideas

What users could do in the app once it supports both dermatoscope and phone-camera photos.

## Main features

- **Use any camera** — Upload a phone photo or a dermatoscope image, and the app figures out which kind it is on its own.
- **Quick check vs. detailed check** — A fast phone-photo check tells you "you should get this looked at," while a dermatoscope photo gives a more confident result.
- **Photo quality help** — If a phone photo is blurry, too dark, or too far away, the app asks you to take it again before checking.
- **Track a mole over time** — Save photos of the same mole and see how it changes (size, color, shape) over the weeks and months.
- **Double-check with two photos** — If you have both a phone and a dermatoscope photo of the same spot, the app combines them and warns you if the results disagree.
- **See how the result was reached** — Open a step-by-step view that shows each stage of the check (cleaned-up photo, detected skin tone, the area the app focused on) so you can understand how it got to the answer.
- **Answer a few quick questions** — Before the check, the app asks simple things like your age, where the spot is on your body, how long you've had it, and whether it has changed, itched, or bled — and uses your answers to give a more accurate, personalized result.

## Extra features

- **Body map** — Mark your moles on a body diagram and keep a personal history of each one.
- **Report for your doctor** — Create a PDF with your photos and results to take to a dermatologist.
- **Honest "not sure" answers** — If a photo doesn't look like something the app can judge, it says so instead of guessing.
- **Easy-to-understand reasons** — Show a simple ABCDE checklist (shape, color, size, etc.) alongside the result.
- **Works fairly for all skin tones** — Show how reliable the result is for your skin tone.
